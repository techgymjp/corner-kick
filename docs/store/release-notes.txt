<en-US>
Corner Kick is here!
• A 3D football game: whip in corners and finish with headers or slides
• Pick the landing spot with the direction ring; hold KICK longer for a higher ball
• After the kick you control the teammate nearest the landing spot
• JUMP for high balls, SLIDE forward, left or right for low ones
• Score from as many of 10 corners as you can. Cheers and fireworks for every goal
• Missed a corner? Watch an ad to take it again (up to 5 times per game)
• NAV help, ranks, score sharing, English and Japanese
