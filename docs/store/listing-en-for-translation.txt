[Full description] (max 4000 characters)
You play soccer in a stadium at night. Your team has a corner kick. Many players wait in front of the goal.
Kick the ball from the corner. Then hit the ball with your head and score a goal. There are 10 corner kicks in one game. How many goals can you score?

■ PLAYER A: TAKE THE CORNER KICK
• Your player (number 10) kicks the ball from the left corner or the right corner.
• Use the round controller to choose where the ball will land. A yellow ring shows the spot.
• Hold the KICK button to kick the ball higher. A short press makes a fast, low ball. A long press makes a high ball.
• The ball curves toward the goal.

■ PLAYER B: MEET THE BALL
• After the kick, you control the teammate who is closest to the landing spot.
• Use the round controller and the DASH button to run to the ball.
• For a high ball, press JUMP. Your player hits the ball with his head.
• For a low ball, slide forward, left or right. Your player hits the ball with his foot.
• Good timing makes a fast and accurate shot. The shot goes to the side away from the goalkeeper.

■ THE OTHER TEAM DEFENDS
• One defender always stays close to your number 9. Pass the ball to another teammate to get away from him.
• A defender near the goal clears low balls.
• If the ball comes too close to the goal, the goalkeeper catches it.
• The goalkeeper jumps to stop your shots. Aim for the corners of the goal.

■ 3D VIEW
• You see the game from behind your player.
• The stadium is full of fans. You see the goal, the net and both teams.
• The game shows the moment you hit the ball in slow motion.
• A small map shows the players and the ball.

■ CHEERS AND FIREWORKS
• The fans play drums and clap. They get louder when the ball comes near the goal.
• When you score a goal, the fans cheer and there are fireworks.
• Headers and shots into the corners give more points. After every 3 goals in a row, you get more fireworks.

■ EASY CONTROLS
• Turn on NAV for help. It shows the path of the ball, where to meet it, and when to press the button.

■ FEATURES
• Ranks from "Sunday League" to "King of the Header".
• Share your score and rank with your friends. The message shows if NAV was on or off.
• If you miss a corner kick, you can watch an ad and try the same corner kick again. You can do this up to 5 times in one game.
• You can play without an internet connection. Ads and sharing need a connection.
• The game is in English and Japanese.
